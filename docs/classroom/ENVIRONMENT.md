## Headless WebGL2 and screenshot mode

- Chromium headless with `--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader --ignore-gpu-blocklist` gives **WebGL2 = true** on SwiftShader (software Vulkan). `tests/e2e/classroom.spec.ts` asserts `webgl2 === true` on every profile.
- Profiles in `playwright.config.ts`: `desktop` 1920×1080, `ipad-keyboard` 1180×820 (no touch), `ipad-touch` 1180×820 (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`).
- **Screenshot mode** for `/classroom.html`: `?shot=N&seed=S&cam=seat|overview|teacher`. It uses a fixed seed and a fixed 1/20 s step per frame, renders N frames, then sets `window.__shotReady = true` and stops. `window.__info` exposes the tick, the sim checksum, WebGL2, draw calls and layout problems.
- Determinism check: the same seed gives the same sim checksum after N frames, and a different seed gives a different one (`classroom sim is deterministic for a seed`).
- Result: **10/10 classroom e2e tests pass** (3 cameras × 3 profiles, plus the determinism test), in about 25 s including the build. The full unit suite passes too (22 files, 240 tests, Places tests included).
- SwiftShader runs on the CPU, so **frame rates measured here mean nothing**. Real numbers have to come from your desktop and iPad (benchmark route, Phase 3).
