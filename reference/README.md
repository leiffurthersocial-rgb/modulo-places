## What I take from them (layout, proportions, materials only)

**Room shell**

- Exposed board-formed **concrete** walls and a square concrete **column** at the window wall. A concrete **beam** crosses under the ceiling.
- **Ceiling**: thin honey-coloured wood-look slats on a dark backing, running across the room. A white **ceiling projector** hangs on a short drop rod.
- **Floor**: dark brown needle-felt **carpet**.
- **Window wall** (right in photo 2): large windows with a tilt-open sash, deep concrete reveals, and a long **radiator grille** in a sill box below them.
- **Door**: dark red/orange-painted door with a steel frame and a lever handle, at the back-left. The **wall clock** (white face, aluminium rim) hangs above it.
- **Sink corner**: a beige mosaic-tile splashback with a small white basin beside the door. Its hard surface makes it a "loud zone" in the game.
- **Yellow pinboard** (large, low-saturation yellow) on the side wall; a dark **chalk/blackboard** at the front in photo 1; a projection/whiteboard area at the front.
- Back wall: a few **framed posters** at about eye height (original placeholders will replace these; nothing is copied).

**Furniture**

- White rectangular laminate **tables**, about 1.3 × 0.65 m, at about 0.72 m height, on thin chrome/steel legs. They are pushed together into a **U** (photo 2 also shows an inner second row, forming a double U).
